Before we begin

Frequently asked
questions.

What we create, how we work and how we can bring your idea to life.

Do you plan intimate events?
Yes. We create intimate gatherings with the same care and creative attention as larger celebrations, from the overall concept to the details that make the occasion feel personal.
Do you offer full event planning?
Yes. We can support an event from the planning stage through to execution. Depending on the brief, this may include developing the concept, building the planning timeline, coordinating suppliers and logistics, managing the details and overseeing the event on the day. Event planning can also be commissioned alongside our styling services.
Do you offer balloon art and balloon installations?
Yes. We create balloon art and bespoke balloon installations, incorporating colour, form and styling to suit the event concept.
Do you style children's parties?
Yes. We style children's celebrations, including themed parties, thoughtful décor, balloon installations and details designed around the occasion.
Do you create date-night experiences?
Yes. We create considered date-night settings, romantic dinners, proposals and personalised experiences for two.
Do you organise picnics?
Yes. We create styled picnic experiences, bringing together the setting, tablescape, florals and finishing details.
Can you work within my budget?
Absolutely. We work with your available budget to shape the scope, creative direction and details of the project. We will help identify the priorities and make considered choices so the investment is used thoughtfully.
Do you create floral arrangements for homes?
Yes. We create bespoke floral arrangements for residential spaces, with compositions considered in relation to the room, palette, scale and setting.
Can I request something that isn't listed on your website?
Of course. The services shown are a guide to our work, not a limit to what we can create. Share your idea with us and we can explore whether it is a fit for the studio.
